From interviews with parents, students and a head of department in Spain and Nepal. A small sample, and a start.

The idea

  • The teacher adds the lesson. Slides or notes. Nothing new to make.
  • The student recaps it aloud. From memory, up to 15 minutes. The conversation stays hidden, so the screen stays quiet.
  • A checker keeps it honest. A separate, strict checker compares the recap with the teacher’s material. Pip never says whether you are right.
  • The teacher sees what stuck. Per student, per idea.

Our bet: knowing you must recap tonight is a reason to listen today. The pilot will test it.
